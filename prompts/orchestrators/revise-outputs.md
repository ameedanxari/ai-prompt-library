Check source completeness in both directions, concrete behavior differences and authorized dispositions, conflicts, closure evidence, safety duties and unknowns. Inspect actual read/mutation target ownership and post-launch lifecycle. Version 2 needs bound companions and applicable architecture; preserve legacy history.

Verify first-proof recovery, milestone prerequisites, scoped variants, test ownership/discovery, real bootstrap and build boundaries. Walk missing input→producer→completion decision→consumer: access gates the producer; completion acceptance gates downstream work. Keep reviews scoped and base/change-order release gates independent.

Use `.ai-prompts/docs/planning-quality.md` for the owner brief, process-inclusive schedule, executable-now summary, lifecycle separation and cold-start evaluation. Source-stated deadlines are constraints, not evidence that estimates fit. No optimistic fixed-budget claim from unknown hours.

Check the shared pre-design, pre-proof and post-proof detail budgets, including hidden detail. Architecture is not a loophole for tasks. Validate current evidence and review authority; challenge copied IDs against actual behavior. A schema pass is not design, proof or release acceptance.

Fix only affected bounded artifacts, retain failure history, and rerun applicable checks. Report residual blockers honestly. Legacy `revise-report.md` is authored by `.ai-prompts/scripts/revise.sh`; never hand-author a passing mechanical report or claim it proves outcome acceptance.
