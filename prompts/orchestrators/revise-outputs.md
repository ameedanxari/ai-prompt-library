# Active-slice Revise Gate

For outcome runs, review the full concise scope ledger plus the bounded active slice; do not require exhaustive tasks for all deferred outcomes. The canonical shared resolver and `validate-delivery.sh` determine applicable scope. Legacy task-schema/phase checks remain in the explicit legacy workflow only.

Run `bash .ai-prompts/scripts/validate-delivery.sh <run-directory> --stage plan` and fix actual failures without hand-authoring success reports. Review:

- Every source requirement, actor, real entry and applicable release duty remains represented; exclusions have authority/provenance and non-waivable duties remain required.
- Unknown defaults choose the smallest applicable platforms and capabilities. Safety/reliability are explicit, without a generic feature inventory.
- First proof reaches a valuable result through actual composition and a reachable recovery path. UI plans include composed-screen captures; headless plans name actual consumers and operations.
- Units are cohesive useful multi-file changes with concrete paths, ownership, dependencies, success/recovery checks, target and evidence. No hidden megaslice or per-file ticket explosion.
- Detailed unimplemented content across all locations meets the five-unit/4,000-word pre-proof or eight-unit/6,000-word post-proof budget. Only evidenced accepted history is excluded. Any extension is bounded and reviewed before expansion.
- Authorization distinguishes execution, required checkpoints, scope/design/release authority and independent review. Required owner or reviewer acceptance cannot be self-granted.
- Evidence is current, nonempty, raw, source/target/configuration-bound and digest-verifiable. Review provenance is resolvable through trusted sources. A validator cannot infer visual quality or authenticate a copied session ID.

Regenerate only the affected bounded artifacts after failure; preserve full scope and decision/evidence history. Re-run appropriate gates. Report residual failures honestly and keep plan-ready pending. A plan-ready gate does not establish proof-accepted, slice-accepted or release-ready. At advancement/release use the corresponding validation stage and semantic review against raw behavior.

For legacy `revise-report.md`, let `.ai-prompts/scripts/revise.sh` author the report. Do not claim its task-schema checks validate outcome evidence or release readiness.
