# Semantic Review and Validation

Mechanical integrity does not prove useful behavior, aesthetic quality or truthful evidence. For outcome runs review the authoritative full scope and the selected slice/release candidate under `.ai-prompts/prompts/orchestrators/rolling-delivery.md`. Existing semantic JSON reports may project findings; they cannot independently override manifest eligibility.

Use `.ai-prompts/prompts/review/` dimensions as applicable: intent fidelity, implementation correctness, functional validation, integration/composition, adversarial cases, evidence quality, user outcome and content experience. Required independent review comes from distinct reviewer context with raw sources/evidence, not a self-review label or copied session ID. Resolve provenance through trusted owner messages, independent session/tool receipts or verifiable external approval bound to reviewed scope/artifact digests. Without access, report unverified and retain pending acceptance.

Reviewers must independently exercise the primary result and inspect raw artifacts. Start from actual entry, reach first value and exercise the relevant recovery/fallback. For visual surfaces inspect captured composed screens at declared sizes/devices and fresh/populated states. For services/SDKs use real consumer invocation; CLI uses installed invocation; data/ML uses representative inputs and assessed outputs; infrastructure uses authorized isolated operation/recovery; documents/media render in the intended consumer. Combine profiles for mixed systems. Fake-provider proof cannot satisfy production integration/release.

Challenge omission of the homepage/entry, broken linked fallback, empty catalogue, stale or cross-environment screenshots, palette-only approval, invented reviewer approval, empty release gates, hidden scope removal and unproved cross-slice prerequisites. Compare the original brief, applicable obligations and the complete release ledger, not just implementer-selected claims. Preserve disagreement and unresolved findings; majority vote never erases a blocker.

For visual design review read every bound authoritative design document and compare its criteria against actual composed entry captures at every required viewport/theme. Assess intent, material/warmth, composition, hierarchy, content density and responsive behavior, not merely tokens/fonts or working navigation. Require same-outcome criterion/context comparisons in current `design-review` evidence and a separate policy-appropriate trusted `design-review` decision bound by `deliveryDesignReviewBinding`. Missing documents/comparisons, wrong route/context, stale images, failures or rejection leave design acceptance pending and block unrelated planning breadth. Design evidence cannot use carry-forward. If no source guidance exists, review the explicitly proposed, digest-bound direction without presenting it as original owner authority. Mechanical validation checks structure, coverage, freshness and provenance; the reviewer must judge visual fidelity.

For each finding record stable ID, source requirements/outcomes/checks, expected and observed behavior, exact target, raw evidence, severity, uncertainty and closure authority. Group repairs into bounded cohesive units; remaining findings stay concise in the ledger. Owner questions are one decision per question with options, recommendation and affected scope. A decided/accepted-risk row is not proof of behavior and cannot waive a hard release obligation or explicit requirement without proper authority.

Validate `plan`, `advance` or `release` using `.ai-prompts/scripts/validate-delivery.sh` as appropriate. Keep plan-ready, design-accepted outcomes, proof-accepted, slice-accepted and release-ready distinct. On relevant change invalidate affected acceptance and dependent eligibility; functional carry-forward needs reviewed dependency scope and retained evidence. No required missing proof may become release-ready.
