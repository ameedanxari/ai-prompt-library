# Semantic Review and Validation

Mechanical integrity does not prove useful behavior, aesthetic quality or truthful evidence. Read original requirements and referenced documents before implementer completion narratives. Reconcile source-to-outcome completeness and outcome-to-source traceability, retaining unresolved obligations. For outcome runs review the authoritative full scope and the selected slice/release candidate under `.ai-prompts/prompts/orchestrators/rolling-delivery.md`. Existing semantic JSON reports may project findings; they cannot independently override manifest eligibility.

Use `.ai-prompts/prompts/review/` dimensions as applicable: intent fidelity, implementation correctness, functional validation, integration/composition, adversarial cases, evidence quality, user outcome and content experience. Required independent review comes from distinct reviewer context with raw sources/evidence, not a self-review label or copied session ID. Resolve provenance through trusted owner messages, independent session/tool receipts or verifiable external approval bound to reviewed scope/artifact digests. Without access, report unverified and retain pending acceptance.

Reviewers must independently exercise the primary result and inspect raw artifacts. Start from actual entry, reach first value and exercise the relevant recovery/fallback. For visual surfaces inspect captured composed screens at declared sizes/devices and fresh/populated states. For services/SDKs use real consumer invocation; CLI uses installed invocation; data/ML uses representative inputs and assessed outputs; infrastructure uses authorized isolated operation/recovery; documents/media render in the intended consumer. Combine profiles for mixed systems. Fake-provider proof cannot satisfy production integration/release.

Challenge omission of the homepage/entry, broken linked fallback, empty catalogue, stale or cross-environment screenshots, palette-only approval, invented reviewer approval, empty release gates, hidden scope removal and unproved cross-slice prerequisites. Compare the original brief, applicable obligations and the complete release ledger, not just implementer-selected claims. Preserve disagreement and unresolved findings; majority vote never erases a blocker.

For visual design review read every bound authoritative design document and compare its criteria against actual composed entry captures at every required viewport/theme. Assess intent, material/warmth, composition, hierarchy, content density and responsive behavior, not merely tokens/fonts or working navigation. Require explicit criterion applicability/exclusions, same-outcome applicable criterion/context comparisons and comparison-v2 `experience` judgments for every context in `design-review` evidence. Record strengths, contrary evidence, rationale and blocking findings; a failed/unverified composed experience cannot pass on token compliance. Source-backed not-applicable rows are excluded from the pass denominator. Require a separate policy-appropriate trusted `design-review` decision bound by `deliveryDesignReviewBinding`. Missing documents/comparisons, wrong route/context, stale images, failures or rejection leave design acceptance pending and block unrelated planning breadth. Visual judgment reuse requires an unchanged `reviewDigest` of source criteria/input dependencies, the original comparison and trusted decision, plus fresh current composed entry captures with exact artifact hashes and matching outcome/route/context. Missing or changed-input judgments require review; unchanged rejected judgments remain blocked until reviewed inputs change. Functional/recovery and release execution proof stays fresh. Human permission to continue does not establish aesthetic acceptance. If no source guidance exists, review the explicitly proposed, digest-bound direction without presenting it as original owner authority. Mechanical validation checks structure, coverage, freshness and provenance; the reviewer must judge visual fidelity.

For each finding record stable ID, source requirements/outcomes/checks, expected and observed behavior, exact target, raw evidence, severity, uncertainty and closure authority. Group repairs into bounded cohesive units; remaining findings stay concise in the ledger. Owner questions are one decision per question with options, recommendation and affected scope. A decided/accepted-risk row is not proof of behavior and cannot waive a hard release obligation or explicit requirement without proper authority.

Validate `plan`, `advance` or `release` using `.ai-prompts/scripts/validate-delivery.sh` as appropriate. Keep plan-ready, design-accepted outcomes, proof-accepted, slice-accepted and release-ready distinct. On relevant change invalidate affected acceptance and dependent eligibility; functional carry-forward needs reviewed dependency scope and retained evidence. Release requires its own trusted `release-review` decision through `deliveryReleaseReviewBinding(scope)`, binding full original-brief/cross-role journey review to current-candidate execution and original-source artifacts. Default `deliveryReviewBinding` can retain functional acceptance judgment for unchanged outcome `reviewDigest`, plan content and raw semantic observation fingerprints across fresh executions; timestamps/IDs/revisions/candidates alone do not change that judgment. Current exact-target execution is still mandatory, changed raw outputs require review, and outputs must never be silently normalized. Design remains separate; release binding uses exact current records/target fingerprints and requires fresh review. Explicit carry-forward evidence bindings pin the full immutable old records. No required missing proof may become release-ready.

Review cadence is focused checks per change, required independent AI review per coherent journey, and full original-brief/cross-role journey review at release or scope change. Do not launch recurring fresh review without a new question, changed input or unresolved finding. Record review input fingerprints and findings; use `planDeliveryReview(scope, kind, outcomeIds, {trustedReviewProvider})` before dispatch: current authenticated approval returns `reuse`, unchanged authenticated rejection returns `blocked`, and only `review` permits a new dispatch. Missing required evidence or current source/scope approval also blocks dispatch. Use `requiredReviewRoles` and the returned `missingRoles` to preserve owner/independent duties; no review counter grants approval. Use the reviewed outcome/shared binding-path impact map to bound affected work; unclassified bindings fall back to the full surface, and execution/release evidence still binds the exact deployment.
