# Design System Review Artifact

## Purpose

Use a token/component catalogue as optional supporting design evidence for the active slice. It cannot substitute for actual composed-screen proof or establish product acceptance.

## Instructions

Reuse source/reference facts, inspect actual visual references and record unavailable material as uncertainty. Generate a concise renderable artifact only when it resolves a concrete design question. Include the tokens/components/states needed for the slice and source provenance. Existing generation/validation helpers may check its structure; they do not grant aesthetic or product approval.

Capture and inspect the actual tested composed application: entry, useful success and relevant recovery, representative sizes/devices and fresh/populated states. Present target/action sequence, raw evidence and limitations at the declared checkpoint. Bind required acceptance to actual scope/artifact digests and trusted owner/reviewer receipts. Component approval cannot authorize unreviewed screen composition.

## Examples

A palette and form-state sheet may accompany booking-screen captures. If the booking link is broken or its recovery is missing, the proof remains unaccepted regardless of catalogue quality. Relevant design/content changes stale affected acceptance and dependent eligibility.
