## Templates

- **notification-channels.md** - Email, SMS, push, and webhook delivery
- **notification-personalization.md** - Content and timing optimization
- **notification-compliance.md** - Opt-in/opt-out and GDPR compliance
- **notification-analytics.md** - Delivery tracking and engagement metrics
- **rich-notifications.md** - Interactive elements and deep linking
- **communication-automation.md** - Trigger-based and drip campaigns
- **enterprise-communications.md** - Approval workflows and brand management
- **real-time-notifications.md** - Instant delivery and presence indicators
