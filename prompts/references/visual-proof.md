# Visual proof

Read and inspect the actual written and visual sources. Preserve their intent, hierarchy, density, material treatment, typography, content and interaction where relevant. If no direction exists, propose a small source-consistent direction within delegated authority; describe it as a proposal. Inaccessible references are unknown, not inspected inspiration.

Build the representative real route on the selected stack with existing primitives where useful. A different prototype stack needs a reason. Tokens and component catalogues support the screen; they do not substitute for early composed experience. Avoid a whole-product design-system phase before showing useful value.

Walk entry → primary action → useful result → reachable recovery with real copy and representative data. Inspect fresh, populated, empty, loading, denied/error and recovery states that actually apply. Check responsive layout on relevant sizes, keyboard/focus behavior and accessible meaning. Images must show the real tested route and current composition. Explain observable strengths and defects against source intent; a row count or aesthetic adjective is not a judgment.

Keep functional behavior, visual quality and authorization distinct. A screen can look right while acting on the wrong record; a working control can miss the requested composition. Review the whole journey, not isolated tokens. Preserve findings and post-fix captures. Changed source, content, components or layout may invalidate affected judgments; unchanged unrelated inputs do not demand a ritual re-review.

Show the user the runnable route/artifact and relevant current captures at declared checkpoints. Do not expose internal run/schema vocabulary as product copy. Use authentic product identity and clearly labelled isolated demonstration data; ordinary accounts must not receive fixtures presented as live data.
